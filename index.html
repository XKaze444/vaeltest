INTRODUCTION

What is Microbiology?

Microbiology is the branch of biology that studies microorganisms, including bacteria, viruses, fungi, protozoa, and microscopic algae.

Although they are too small to be seen with the naked eye, microorganisms are everywhere. They play important roles in ecosystems, human health, food production, and biotechnology.
